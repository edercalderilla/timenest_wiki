Questions

## Quick questions.

### Do my clients need to download anything?

No. They book from a link in their browser and get their confirmations and reminders straight over WhatsApp.

### How do WhatsApp reminders work?

TimeNest sends them automatically based on your business settings. You decide how far in advance each appointment gets a reminder.

### What happens if I run out of included messages?

On TimeNest Free you get 50 messages a month: once you hit the limit, reminders pause until your next cycle. On Pro you get 350 messages a month and, if you need more, extras are billed by usage.

### Can I have several staff members or locations?

On Free you work with 1 location and up to 2 team members. On Pro you get unlimited staff and up to 3 locations, each with their own schedule and services.

### Do I need a card to start?

No. You can create your account and start on TimeNest Free without registering a card.

### What makes TimeNest different from other booking platforms?

Pricing in Mexican pesos with no lock-in contracts: switch plans or cancel whenever you want. TimeNest takes no commission per appointment: what you charge your clients is yours. And your clients download nothing: they book from your link and get everything on WhatsApp.
