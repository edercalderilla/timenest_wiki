# Who is behind TimeNest

TimeNest is a service developed and operated by **Eder Jahziel Calderilla Rodríguez**, from Mexico, for businesses that run on appointments: barbershops, salons, spas, private practices, and any business that works by reservation.

It was born from a simple observation: in Mexico, a small business's schedule lives on WhatsApp. That's where appointments arrive, get confirmed, and get canceled — all by hand, message by message, while the owner tries to serve clients. TimeNest doesn't fight that reality: it automates it. Your clients book from your link and the WhatsApp follow-up goes out on its own.

## How we decide what to build

### WhatsApp first

Your clients won't download another app or check their email. Messages arrive where they already are.

### Pricing in pesos, no fine print

Plans in MXN, no lock-in contracts, and no commission per appointment. What you charge your clients is yours.

### Your clients' data belongs to your business

TimeNest processes it only to run your schedule, formally and in writing: our privacy policy spells it out.
