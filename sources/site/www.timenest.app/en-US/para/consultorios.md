## What it solves in a practice

### The front desk lives on confirmation calls

Calling patient by patient the day before eats the morning. TimeNest sends the confirmation at booking and the reminder before the visit, automatically.

### No-shows that leave the slot empty

A patient who doesn't show is lost consultation time. The reminder arrives on WhatsApp, and if the patient cancels, the slot frees up instantly to be reassigned.

### A schedule shared across professionals

If several professionals see patients at the practice, each has their own hours, visit types, and absences, and the front desk sees everything on one screen.

## Private practice FAQs

### Can my patients confirm their attendance?

Yes. From their appointment's WhatsApp message, patients can confirm they'll attend or cancel. The status shows on your schedule, and a cancellation frees the slot instantly.

### Does it work for a practice with several specialists?

Yes. Each professional has their own hours, visit types, and absences. Free covers up to 2 professionals; Pro has unlimited staff and up to 3 locations.

### What happens with my patients' data?

Your practice is the controller of your patients' data; TimeNest acts as processor and uses it solely to run your schedule and send your appointment messages, as described in the privacy policy.

### Do patients need to install anything?

No. They book from your link in the browser and receive their confirmation and reminder on WhatsApp. For the practice, the panel also runs in the browser.
