TimeNest is an appointment system for beauty studios: you publish your booking link, clients pick a service and a time from their phone, and they get their confirmation and reminder on WhatsApp. Every service is set up with its real duration — a full lash set doesn't block the same time as a refill — so your day's schedule closes exactly. Start free with 1 studio, up to 2 people, and 50 messages per month.

If you work from home or rent a station, your link is your front desk: it books the appointments, answers the 'got a slot?' messages, and does the follow-up while you work.

## What it solves in a beauty studio

### Back-to-back services all day

When one appointment runs late or doesn't show, the whole day shifts. With real per-service durations and reminders that actually arrive, your day's schedule holds.

### Refills and full sets take different times

A nail refill isn't a full set. Each service has its duration and reference price, and your link only offers times where the chosen service fits.

### Answering messages with your hands busy

In the middle of a lash lift you can't pick up the phone. Your link books on its own, and confirmations and reminders go out without you touching your phone.

## Beauty studio FAQs

### I work alone from home. Is TimeNest right for me?

Yes. The free plan includes your schedule, your booking link, and 50 WhatsApp messages per month, no card required. Your link works as your front desk while you work.

### Can I handle services with different durations?

Yes. Each service — full set, refill, facial, waxing — is registered with its duration and reference price, and your link only offers times where the full service fits.

### Do my clients need to download an app?

No. They book from your link in the browser and get their confirmation and reminder on WhatsApp, which they already use every day.

### What happens if a client cancels?

They can cancel from their appointment's message, without texting you. The slot frees up immediately on your schedule for another client to take.
