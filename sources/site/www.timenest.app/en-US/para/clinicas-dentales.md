## What it solves in a dental clinic

### No-shows on long treatments

A patient who misses a 90-minute root canal leaves a gap that's hard to fill. The reminder arrives on WhatsApp before the appointment, and a cancellation frees the full block instantly.

### Treatments with very different durations

Evaluation, cleaning, filling, extraction: each visit type has its duration in TimeNest, and the schedule only offers times where the full treatment fits.

### The front desk confirms patient by patient

Calling the day before to confirm eats the morning. The confirmation goes out at booking and the reminder before the visit, automatically.

## Dental clinic FAQs

### Can I set different durations per visit type?

Yes. Each visit type — evaluation, cleaning, filling, root canal — is registered with its duration and reference price, and the schedule blocks the treatment's full time.

### Can my patients confirm their attendance?

Yes. From their appointment's WhatsApp message, patients can confirm they'll attend or cancel. The status shows on your schedule, and a cancellation frees the slot instantly.

### Does it work for a clinic with several dentists?

Yes. Each dentist or specialist has their own hours, visit types, and absences. Free covers up to 2 professionals; Pro has unlimited staff and up to 3 locations.

### What happens with my patients' data?

Your clinic is the controller of your patients' data; TimeNest acts as processor and uses it solely to run your schedule and send your appointment messages, as described in the privacy policy.
