TimeNest is a booking app for hair salons: you publish your booking page, clients pick a service, a stylist, and a time, and they get a WhatsApp confirmation and reminder. Every service has its own duration and price — a color job doesn't block the same time as a haircut — and every stylist manages their own hours and services. When the team grows, TimeNest Pro adds unlimited staff and up to 3 locations, with the whole salon's schedule in a single view.

## What it solves in a salon

### Services with different durations

A three-hour balayage can't be booked like a forty-minute cut. Every service in TimeNest has its duration, and the schedule only offers times where it actually fits.

### Double bookings between stylists

When the schedule lives in a notebook or a chat, two clients end up at the same time with the same person. With per-stylist schedules, everyone only gets appointments they can take.

### Confirming appointment by appointment, by hand

Texting every client the day before eats up hours. TimeNest sends the confirmation at booking and the reminder before the appointment, automatically.

## Built for the salon's rhythm

### Schedule and services per stylist

Each team member defines which services they take and when. Absences and exceptions are tracked per person too.

### Duration and price per service

Cut, color, styling, nails: each with its own time and reference price visible on the booking page.

### WhatsApp follow-up

Confirmation at booking, reminder before the appointment, and cancellation from the message itself — no back-and-forth.

### Grow to more locations

With Pro, up to 3 locations with their own hours and teams, and unlimited staff at each one.

## Salon FAQs

### Can I have several stylists with different schedules?

Yes. Each team member has their own hours, services, and absences. Free covers up to 2 people; Pro has unlimited staff.

### How do I handle services with different durations?

Each service is set up with its duration and price. The booking page only offers times where the full service fits in the chosen stylist's schedule.

### Can my clients cancel or change their appointment on their own?

They can check and cancel from their appointment's WhatsApp message, without texting you. The slot frees up instantly; to rebook, they simply reserve again from your link.
