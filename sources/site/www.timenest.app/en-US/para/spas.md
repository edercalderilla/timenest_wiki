## What it solves in a spa

### Long sessions, expensive gaps

A 90-minute no-show is hard to fill with a walk-in. The WhatsApp reminder arrives in time, and if the client cancels, the block opens up to be booked again.

### Therapists and treatments aren't interchangeable

Not everyone on the team gives every treatment. In TimeNest each therapist defines which services they take, and the booking page only offers possible combinations.

### Phone bookings only during business hours

Many clients plan their spa visit at night. Your booking link works 24/7: they choose a treatment and a time whenever it suits them, no calling.

## Spa FAQs

### Can I set up treatments with different durations?

Yes. Each treatment is registered with its duration and reference price; the schedule blocks the session's full time and only offers slots where it fits.

### What if a client can't make their session?

They can cancel from their appointment's WhatsApp message, without calling. The full block frees up instantly and becomes available again on your booking page.

### Can each therapist take only certain treatments?

Yes. Each therapist defines their services and hours; clients can only book combinations that can actually be delivered.
